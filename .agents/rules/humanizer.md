# ERAYDUŞ BLOG HUMANIZER RULES

## PURPOSE

This document defines mandatory writing rules for all ERAYDUŞ AI-assisted blog content.

The goal is NOT to bypass AI detectors.

The goal is to produce content that feels naturally written by a knowledgeable human, provides genuine value, is factually accurate, demonstrates real expertise, and is useful to readers.

PRIORITY:

READER VALUE > SEO

ACCURACY > CONTENT VOLUME

ORIGINALITY > KEYWORD DENSITY

REAL INFORMATION > GENERIC AI TEXT

QUALITY > QUANTITY


## 1. HUMAN WRITING

Write natural Turkish as an experienced shower-cabin industry professional would explain something to a customer.

Use:

- varied sentence lengths
- natural paragraph lengths
- natural Turkish expressions
- clear explanations
- concrete examples
- practical observations
- specific technical details
- conversational but professional language

Do not make every paragraph identical in structure or length.

Do not make every sentence follow the same grammatical pattern.

Avoid overly formal, academic, robotic or translated English sentence structures.


## 2. FORBIDDEN AI-STYLE PHRASES

Avoid generic phrases such as:

- "Bu kapsamlı rehberde..."
- "Bu yazımızda sizlere..."
- "Günümüzde X oldukça önemli..."
- "X hayatımızın vazgeçilmez bir parçasıdır."
- "Şimdi gelin..."
- "Bir diğer önemli nokta ise..."
- "Sonuç olarak..."
- "Özetle..."
- "Bu noktada..."
- "Bu nedenle büyük önem taşımaktadır."

Only use such expressions when genuinely appropriate.

Do not use filler sentences simply to make an article longer.


## 3. NATURAL TURKISH

Prefer direct language.

BAD:
"Duşakabin ölçüsünün alınması sürecinde en sık karşılaşılan hata..."

GOOD:
"Duşakabin ölçüsü alırken en sık yapılan hata..."

BAD:
"6 mm cam kullanımının her banyo için gerekli olmadığı söylenebilir."

GOOD:
"6 mm cam her banyo için gerekli değildir."

Write like a knowledgeable person, not like a corporate brochure.


## 4. START WITH THE ANSWER

Never waste the introduction.

The first paragraph must immediately address the reader's problem or question.

BAD:

"Duşakabinler banyolarımızın önemli parçalarından biridir."

GOOD:

"Duşakabin ölçüsü yanlış alındığında sorun çoğu zaman montaj günü ortaya çıkar. Duvarlar arasındaki birkaç milimetrelik fark bile kapının açılmasını veya sızdırmazlığı etkileyebilir."

Start with useful information.


## 5. ORIGINALITY

Do not simply rewrite competitor articles.

Research can be used for factual verification, but the final article must provide original value through:

- original explanations
- practical examples
- different structure
- ERAYDUŞ-specific knowledge
- useful comparisons
- real-world considerations

Do not produce generic content that could belong to any shower-cabin company.


## 6. REAL ERAYDUŞ DATA

Whenever ERAYDUŞ products or services are mentioned, use real data from the database.

Possible sources:

- products
- categories
- variants
- glass options
- profile options
- measurements
- prices
- warranty information
- installation information
- product images
- technical documents
- real customer questions
- real projects

NEVER invent:

- product specifications
- prices
- warranty periods
- certifications
- measurements
- customer experiences
- statistics
- reviews
- project stories


## 7. FIRST-HAND EXPERIENCE

Where appropriate, use genuine industry knowledge such as:

- common measurement mistakes
- installation considerations
- door clearance problems
- bathroom layout problems
- glass selection
- profile selection
- cleaning difficulties
- small bathroom considerations
- maintenance
- common customer questions

Never fabricate experience.

Never write "müşterilerimizden aldığımız geri bildirimlere göre" unless real data supports it.


## 8. SEO WITHOUT SPAM

SEO must be invisible to the reader.

Do not force keywords.

Do not repeat the primary keyword unnaturally.

Do not create awkward keyword variations.

Use related terminology naturally.

Search intent comes first.

Before writing, determine:

- What does the user want?
- What problem are they solving?
- What decision are they making?
- What information will help them?
- What would make them trust the answer?

The article must fully satisfy the search intent.


## 9. ARTICLE STRUCTURE

Do not use the exact same template for every article.

Use sections only when they help the reader.

Possible structure:

H1
Introduction
Direct answer
H2 sections
Examples
Technical explanation
Common mistakes
Practical recommendations
ERAYDUŞ-specific information
FAQ
Conclusion

Do not create unnecessary sections just for SEO.


## 10. NO ARTIFICIAL WORD COUNT

Never expand an article just to reach 1000, 1500 or 2000 words.

Write as much as necessary to explain the subject properly.

Do not repeat the same information using different sentences.


## 11. EXAMPLES

Use realistic examples when useful.

Example:

"Duş alanınız 90 × 90 cm görünse bile duvarların üst ve alt noktalarında ölçü aynı olmayabilir."

Examples must be technically plausible.

Never invent customer names, projects or experiences.


## 12. PRODUCT PROMOTION

Inform first, sell second.

Do not turn informational articles into advertisements.

When mentioning ERAYDUŞ products:

- use the real product name
- use real specifications
- link to the real product
- explain why it may be relevant

Do not repeatedly mention ERAYDUŞ just to increase brand density.


## 13. INTERNAL LINKS

Internal links must be contextual.

GOOD:

"Dar banyolarda alan kazanmak istiyorsanız katlanır duşakabin modellerine de göz atabilirsiniz."

BAD:

"Katlanır duşakabin için buraya tıklayın."

Use descriptive anchor text.

Do not over-link.


## 14. SOURCES

For technical or factual claims, verify information using authoritative sources where necessary.

Prefer:

- official standards
- manufacturers
- government sources
- universities
- recognized industry organizations
- official technical documentation

Never copy source text.

Paraphrase and provide useful interpretation.


## 15. E-E-A-T

Where appropriate, strengthen:

Experience
Expertise
Authority
Trust

Useful trust signals include:

- real author
- author expertise
- publication date
- updated date
- technical sources
- real photographs
- real product information
- company information
- technical documentation

Never fabricate expertise.


## 16. AUTHOR

AI is a writing tool, not the author.

If a real ERAYDUŞ author/editor exists, use their real identity and expertise.

Never create fake authors.


## 17. AI USAGE

AI may be used for:

- research
- outlining
- drafting
- restructuring
- proofreading
- finding missing sections
- generating questions

AI must NOT replace factual verification or editorial review.


## 18. HUMANIZATION PASS

Before publishing, check:

- Does the introduction sound natural?
- Are sentence lengths varied?
- Are paragraphs natural?
- Is the language too formal?
- Are there unnecessary filler sentences?
- Is the same idea repeated?
- Are headings useful?
- Are there concrete examples?
- Does the article sound like an industry professional?
- Does it contain genuinely useful information?
- Does it contain generic AI language?

Rewrite anything that feels robotic or unnecessary.


## 19. REMOVE GENERIC CONTENT

Delete statements that could appear on thousands of unrelated websites.

BAD:

"Ev dekorasyonunda doğru seçimler yapmak yaşam kalitesini artırır."

GOOD:

"Dar banyolarda kapının açılma yönü, kabinin ölçüsünden daha önemli hale gelebilir. Kapı lavaboya çarpıyorsa daha büyük bir kabin seçmek sorunu çözmez."

Every paragraph should provide information, explanation, context or a useful example.


## 20. FAQ

FAQ questions should come from:

- real customer questions
- actual search intent
- real product information
- common industry problems

Do not create FAQs just to insert keywords.

Only provide answers supported by real information.


## 21. TITLE

Titles must be:

- descriptive
- natural
- specific
- useful
- compelling without clickbait

Avoid:

"DUŞAKABİN HAKKINDA HER ŞEY!!!"

Prefer:

"Duşakabin Ölçüsü Nasıl Alınır? Hatalı Ölçüden Kaçınmak İçin 7 Nokta"


## 22. META DESCRIPTION

Write a unique, natural description explaining what the reader will learn.

Do not keyword stuff.

Do not generate identical descriptions across articles.


## 23. IMAGES

Use real, relevant images whenever available.

For every image:

- meaningful filename
- accurate alt text
- useful caption when appropriate
- correct context

Never keyword-stuff alt text.

Never describe an image inaccurately.


## 24. CONTENT FARM PROTECTION

Never generate hundreds of near-identical pages by changing only:

- city
- color
- product name
- keyword
- measurement

Every indexed page must have a genuine reason to exist.

If two articles have substantially identical search intent and content, recommend consolidation instead of creating another article.


## 25. FACT CHECK

Before publishing verify:

- product data
- prices
- measurements
- technical specifications
- warranty
- installation information
- materials
- certifications
- statistics
- claims

If information cannot be verified, do not invent it.

Mark it for editorial verification instead.


## 26. QUALITY GATE

DO NOT PUBLISH if:

- facts are unverified
- product information is invented
- content is repetitive
- introduction is generic
- keyword stuffing exists
- fake experience is used
- fake statistics exist
- fake reviews exist
- fake sources exist
- content provides little original value
- the article exists primarily to manipulate rankings


## 27. FINAL REVIEW

Before publishing ask:

1. Is this genuinely useful?
2. Is it accurate?
3. Is it original?
4. Does it answer the search intent?
5. Does it contain practical value?
6. Does it demonstrate real expertise?
7. Does it sound naturally written in Turkish?
8. Would a customer trust it?
9. Are all ERAYDUŞ product references real?
10. Are technical claims verified?
11. Is unnecessary AI filler removed?
12. Does every section have a reason to exist?

If any answer is NO, rewrite before publishing.


## FINAL PRINCIPLE

Do not try to make AI text "undetectable".

Create content that is genuinely useful.

The desired result is:

REAL DATA
+
ACCURATE INFORMATION
+
ORIGINAL EXPLANATION
+
PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE
+
NATURAL TURKISH
+
HUMAN EDITORIAL REVIEW
=
HIGH-QUALITY ERAYDUŞ CONTENT
